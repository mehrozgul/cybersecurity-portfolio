# Botium Toys Security Recommendations

## Objective

The following recommendations are based on the identified security risks, missing controls, and compliance gaps in the Botium Toys security assessment.

## Priority 1 — Implement Least Privilege

Botium Toys should implement the principle of least privilege.

Employees should only have access to the data and systems required for their job responsibilities.

**Priority:** High

**Reason:** All employees currently have access to internally stored data and may be able to access sensitive customer information.

---

## Priority 2 — Encrypt Sensitive Data

Botium Toys should implement encryption to protect customers' credit card information and other sensitive data.

**Priority:** High

**Reason:** Credit card information is currently not encrypted, creating a significant confidentiality risk.

---

## Priority 3 — Implement Backups and Disaster Recovery

The company should create regular backups of critical data and establish a disaster recovery plan.

**Priority:** High

**Reason:** Botium Toys currently has no backups of critical data and no disaster recovery plan.

---

## Priority 4 — Strengthen Password Security

Botium Toys should strengthen its password policy and implement a centralized password management system.

Recommended improvements include:

- Stronger password requirements
- Password complexity requirements
- Centralized password management
- Controlled password reset and recovery processes

**Priority:** High

**Reason:** The existing password policy is weak and there is no centralized password management system.

---

## Priority 5 — Implement an Intrusion Detection System

Botium Toys should deploy an Intrusion Detection System (IDS) to monitor network activity and identify suspicious or anomalous traffic.

**Priority:** High

**Reason:** The company currently has no IDS.

---

## Priority 6 — Improve Access Control and Separation of Duties

The company should establish formal access control policies and implement separation of duties.

**Priority:** High

**Reason:** Access controls related to least privilege and separation of duties have not been implemented.

---

## Priority 7 — Improve Legacy System Management

Botium Toys should establish a regular schedule for monitoring and maintaining legacy systems.

The company should also clearly define intervention procedures for outdated systems.

**Priority:** Medium

**Reason:** Legacy systems are monitored and maintained, but there is no regular schedule and intervention methods are unclear.

---

## Priority 8 — Improve Asset Management and Classification

Botium Toys should properly identify, classify, and inventory its assets and sensitive data.

**Priority:** High

**Reason:** The company currently has inadequate asset management and does not have sufficient information about which assets may be at risk.

---

## Final Recommendation

Botium Toys should prioritize access control, encryption, backups, disaster recovery, password security, IDS implementation, and asset management.

Addressing these weaknesses will reduce the company's overall risk and improve its security posture and compliance with relevant best practices.
